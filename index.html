Questions

Before you get started.

Do I need to be fit before starting online coaching?

No. Coaching is built around your current ability, experience, goals and the equipment you have available.

Can Claire coach me for HYROX?

Yes. HYROX and hybrid coaching can combine strength, running, conditioning and race-specific preparation around your current level.

Do I need a full gym?

Not necessarily. Your programme can be built around the equipment you realistically have access to.

Do you offer nutrition support?

Nutrition guidance can form part of the coaching support alongside training, check-ins and accountability.

What about face-to-face personal training?

Claire plans to add 1:1 location-based personal training in the future once she has a suitable training space. The current focus is online coaching.
