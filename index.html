Questions

Do I need to be fit before starting personal training?

No. Training can be built around your current ability, experience and goals, so beginners can start at an appropriate level.

Do you offer online personal training?

Yes. Claire Louise Fitness offers online coaching alongside in-person personal training in Cambridgeshire.

What happens in the free consultation?

It is a relaxed chat about your goals, training history, availability and what kind of coaching may suit you best.

Can I start if I'm nervous about the gym?

Yes. Sessions can start with the basics and progress at a pace that feels manageable.
